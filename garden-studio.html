The studio

Stillfield’s approach starts with a simple question: how do you want to spend time outside?

From first sketch to planting plan

We begin with how you use the garden across a week. Morning light, a sheltered seat and the path from the kitchen often shape the first sketch.

We compare layout options before moving into materials and planting. The final plan brings those decisions together so you can discuss installation with a contractor.
